# How Long Does It Take to Read the Bible?

**Reading the entire Bible takes most people about 51 to 75 hours.** At an average adult reading speed, that's about 51 hours; Crossway's estimate for a typical Bible reader is 74 hours and 28 minutes. Either way, **about 8 to 12 minutes a day is enough to read the whole Bible in a year.**

| | Chapters | Verses | Words (BSB) | Reading time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Old Testament (39 books) | 929 | 23,145 | 556,257 | about 39–57 hours |
| New Testament (27 books) | 260 | 7,957 | 172,439 | about 12–18 hours |
| **Whole Bible (66 books)** | **1,189** | **31,102** | **728,696** | **about 51–75 hours** |

## Why estimates vary

Reading time depends on three things:

* **Your reading speed.** A meta-analysis of 190 studies by psychologist Marc Brysbaert found that adults silently read non-fiction at an average of about **238 words per minute**. The lower figures on this page use that rate.
* **How you read.** Most people read Scripture more slowly than a novel — pausing to reflect, re-reading, or taking notes. Crossway's estimate of about 74½ hours for the whole Bible reflects a slower, more typical pace.
* **Your translation.** Word counts differ between translations. The counts here come from the Berean Standard Bible (BSB), a modern public-domain translation. Older translations such as the King James Version have more words (about 783,000) and archaic language that many people read more slowly.

## Quick facts

* **Longest book:** Psalms — 150 chapters and about 2 hr 45 min of reading. By word count, Jeremiah is longer.
* **Shortest books:** 2 John and 3 John take about a minute each; Philemon, Jude, and Obadiah each take under 5 minutes.
* **The four Gospels** take about 5½ hours together at an average reading speed.
* **Longest chapter:** Psalm 119 (176 verses). **Shortest chapter:** Psalm 117 (2 verses).
