# How to Read the Bible in 2 Years

**To read the whole Bible in 2 years, read about 1½ chapters — roughly 43 verses, or 4 to 6 minutes — a day.** It's a realistic, sustainable pace for busy seasons, new readers, and anyone who wants time to slow down and reflect.

A one-year plan asks for 3 to 4 chapters a day. If you've tried that and fallen behind, a two-year plan might be exactly what you need. Consistency matters more than speed: research from the Center for Bible Engagement associates reading the Bible **four or more days a week** with the greatest life impact, and a shorter daily reading makes that easier to sustain.

## How much to read each day

| Daily target | To finish in 2 years (730 days) |
|---|---:|
| Chapters | about 1½ a day (about 11 a week) |
| Verses | about 43 a day |
| Time | about 4–6 minutes a day |

Only reading five days a week? Read about 2¼ chapters (60 verses) on each reading day, and you'll still finish in two years.

## A sample 2-year schedule

This schedule reads through the Bible in order, alternating between Old and New Testament sections so you reach the Gospels early.

| Months | Read | Chapters |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Matthew and Mark | 44 |
| 1–5 | Genesis – Deuteronomy | 187 |
| 5–7 | Luke, John, and Acts | 73 |
| 7–12 | Joshua – Esther | 249 |
| 12–13 | Romans – Galatians | 51 |
| 13–18 | Job – Song of Songs | 243 |
| 18 | Ephesians – Philemon | 36 |
| 18–23 | Isaiah – Malachi | 250 |
| 23–24 | Hebrews – Revelation | 56 |

At about 50 chapters a month, each section flows into the next. It doesn't need to be exact — adjust as you go.

## Why a 2-year plan works

* **More time to reflect.** At about 5 minutes a day, you have time to re-read a verse, write a note, or pray about what you read.
* **Easier to catch up.** Falling a day or two behind costs just a few minutes to make up.
* **Builds a lasting habit.** A small, repeatable action done in a consistent context is how habits form. Read more in [How to Build a Strong Bible Reading Habit](/about/how-to--build-a-strong-bible-reading-habit).

## Tips for reading the Bible in 2 years

* **Pair it with study.** With a shorter daily reading, it's easy to add a question, a cross-reference, or a note. See [7 Ways to Organize Your Personal Bible Study](/about/how-to--organize-your-personal-bible-study).
* **Mark milestones.** Celebrate finishing each book. My Bible Log awards a star for every book you complete.
* **Speed up when you can.** On days with more time, read ahead. You might finish early.
