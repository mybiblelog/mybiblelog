# How to Read the Bible in 6 Months

**To read the whole Bible in 6 months, read about 6½ chapters — roughly 171 verses, or 17 to 25 minutes — every day.** That's twice the pace of a one-year plan, and about as long as a TV episode each day.

Reading the Bible in six months is a great challenge if you've read through the Bible before, want to see its big story more clearly, or have a season with more time to read, like a sabbatical, a summer, or Lent through autumn.

## How much to read each day

| Daily target | To finish in 6 months (182 days) |
|---|---:|
| Chapters | about 6½ a day (46 a week) |
| Verses | about 171 a day |
| Time | about 17–25 minutes a day |

The Bible has 1,189 chapters and 31,102 verses. At an average adult reading speed of 238 words per minute, the whole Bible takes about 51 hours. A more typical, reflective pace takes about 74 hours. See [How Long Does It Take to Read the Bible?](/about/guide--how-long-does-it-take-to-read-the-bible) for a book-by-book breakdown.

Because chapters vary so much in length, a daily **verse** goal of about 171 gives you a steadier reading time than counting chapters.

## A sample 6-month schedule

This schedule reads the Bible in order at about 200 chapters a month.

| Month | Read | Chapters |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Genesis – Judges | 232 |
| 2 | Ruth – Nehemiah | 194 |
| 3 | Esther – Psalms | 202 |
| 4 | Proverbs – Ezekiel | 222 |
| 5 | Daniel – Acts | 196 |
| 6 | Romans – Revelation | 143 |

Month 6 is lighter, which leaves you room to catch up on missed days. If you'd rather mix the Old and New Testaments, read a few chapters from each every day, or see other options in [Bible Reading Plans Compared](/about/guide--bible-reading-plans-compared).

## Tips for reading the Bible in 6 months

* **Split your reading.** Two 10-minute sessions (morning and evening) are often easier than one 20-minute block.
* **Listen on busy days.** An audio Bible during a commute or workout keeps you on pace. Log what you listened to so it counts.
* **Use a weekly target.** Aim for about 46 chapters (or 1,200 verses) a week, so a busy day can be made up on the weekend.
* **Keep moving through hard sections.** Long genealogies and legal sections go faster than you expect. Note questions and come back to them later.
* **Track your progress.** Research on goal pursuit shows that monitoring your progress makes you more likely to finish. See our tips for a [strong Bible reading habit](/about/how-to--build-a-strong-bible-reading-habit).

## Not sure 6 months is right for you?

* **A gentler pace:** [Read the Bible in a year](/about/how-to--read-the-bible-in-a-year) — about 3 to 4 chapters a day.
* **Even gentler:** [Read the Bible in 2 years](/about/how-to--read-the-bible-in-2-years) — about 1½ chapters a day.
* **A shorter goal:** [Read the New Testament in 90 days](/about/how-to--read-the-new-testament-in-90-days).
