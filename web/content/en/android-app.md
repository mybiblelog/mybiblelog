# My Bible Log for Android

My Bible Log is now a native Android app — and it's the fastest, most flexible way to track your Bible reading yet. Log chapters in seconds, take it with you anywhere, and pick up right where you left off on the web.

<content-google-play-store-link-banner></content-google-play-store-link-banner>

## Log Your Reading Anywhere — Even Offline

No signal? No problem. Mark chapters as read and take notes even with no internet connection.

* Log entries and notes work fully offline — no waiting on a connection
* Everything syncs automatically the moment you're back online

## Try It Before You Sign Up

Curious but not ready to commit? Tap **I'm New** when the app first opens to start logging your reading right away — no email, no password, no mybiblelog.com account required.

* Start tracking your reading in seconds
* Create your free account whenever you're ready
* Your reading log carries over the moment you sign in

## Free. No Ads. No Subscriptions.

Just like the web app, My Bible Log for Android is completely free — no ads, no premium tier, no in-app purchases.
